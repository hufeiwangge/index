大枫子油：驱避与修复的双重作用机制

Chaulmoogra Oil: Dual Mechanisms of Repellency and Post-Bite Repair

本研究聚焦大枫子油中独特的环戊烯基脂肪酸 (CPFAs)。不同于传统易挥发驱蚊剂，它在皮肤表面形成持久的触觉防护屏障，并能调控叮咬后的局部皮肤组织修复。
This study focuses on the unique Cyclopentenyl Fatty Acids (CPFAs) in Chaulmoogra Oil. Unlike volatile repellents, it forms a long-lasting tactile barrier on the skin and modulates post-bite epidermal repair.

01 前沿防御：接触性拒避与物理掩蔽

Advanced Defense: Contact Avoidance & Physical Masking

触觉拒食效应 [1]

Tactile Feeding Deterrence

当蚊虫接触涂抹区域时，其跗节化学感受器受到 CPFAs 刺激会引发逃避反射，显著降低刺吸尝试。

Tarsal chemoreceptors trigger avoidance reflexes upon contact with CPFAs, significantly reducing biting attempts.

气味信号掩蔽 [2]

Odor Signal Masking

高沸点植物脂质膜可阻断人体二氧化碳及乳酸散发，实现物理信号掩蔽。

The high-boiling lipid membrane blocks the emission of human volatiles (lactate/CO₂), achieving signal masking.

02 深度修复：叮咬后的药理干预

Deep Repair: Post-Bite Pharmacological Intervention

🧬

抗炎镇痛

Anti-inflammation & Analgesia [3]

大风子油酸可抑制组胺释放，降低局部水肿与灼热感，缓解瘙痒。

Chaulmoogric acid inhibits histamine release, reducing edema, burning sensations, and itch.

广谱抑菌

Broad-spectrum Antibacterial [4]

具备抑菌活性，有效抑制金黄色葡萄球菌，预防抓挠引发的继发感染。

Inhibits Staphylococcus aureus, preventing secondary skin infections caused by scratching.

屏障修复

Skin Barrier Repair [5]

黄酮木脂素促进角质细胞代谢，协同基质脂肪酸修复受损物理屏障。

Flavonolignans promote keratinocyte metabolism and restore the physical skin barrier.

● 配伍应用与注意事项

Formulation & Usage Precautions

  • ✔ 复方协同 / Synergistic Formulation [6]：可与挥发性精油（如香茅油）配伍，兼顾空间驱避与长效接触防护。
    Combines with volatile oils (e.g., Citronella Oil) to provide both spatial repellency and long-lasting contact protection.
  • ! 外用注意 / General Precaution：仅供外用，严禁口服。
    For external use only; strictly DO NOT ingest.
